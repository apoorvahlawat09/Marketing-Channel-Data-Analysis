# Marketing-Channel-Data-Analysis

Analysis of a sample sales and marketing channel dataset to find key business insights using Microsoft Excel.

• Evaluated 10K transactions worth ₹60.2M revenue across sales channels, regions, marketing channels and discount levels to answer key business questions around channel performance, regional viability, marketing effectiveness, promotional impact and the single highest-priority growth opportunity.

• Applied Excel Pivot Tables, conditional aggregation, segmentation, cross-tab analysis, ranking, percentage-share analysis and correlation analysis to compare revenue, profit margins, return rates, and discount-to-volume partnerships.

• Answered key business Qs such as:

Which sales channel is performing better — Online or In-Store?

The business is considering cutting its lowest-revenue region. Is that the right call? What does the data actually tell you?

Which marketing channel is delivering the best results? What would you recommend doubling down on — and what would you cut?

A colleague claims: "Discount campaigns are working great — our highest-discount transactions have the most units sold." Do you agree?

If you had to walk the CEO through the single most important insight from this dataset in 60 seconds, what would it be — and what action would you recommend?
